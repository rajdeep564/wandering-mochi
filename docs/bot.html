About MochiBot

If you found this link in your server logs, here is what happened.

What she does

MochiBot identifies itself with the user agent MochiBot/0.1. Once a day it visits up to three sites on the guest list and reads robots.txt and the homepage. It does not follow internal links or run JavaScript, and it never stores page text.

If you never invited her

When a guest links to your site, she may read your robots.txt and homepage once to look for an invitation, at most once every 30 days and at most five linked sites per guest. Without one she leaves, and your site is never published. The repository keeps only a one-way hash of the host name, so it can remember not to come back early.

How to block her

User-agent: MochiBot
Disallow: /

To stop visits from an adopted site, remove the script or the invitation tag. On her next check she removes your site, its diary entries and its badge.

What counts as an invitation

Either the Mochi script on your homepage, or this tag:

<meta name="mochi" content="welcome" />

The paw score

She gives one paw each for a page title, description, mobile viewport tag, language tag and main heading. Her written reviews are playful, and no AI service reads your site.
