An open source pixel cat for your website

She reviews websites. Mostly by sitting on them.

Mochi lives on your page, follows your cursor and naps wherever is least convenient. Once a day she wanders off to other invited sites and comes home with a souvenir and a very honest review.

      Adoption papers

      Adopt Mochi

      Installs in one line. Ignores you in zero.

      Certificate of adoption No. 0001 · one cat, pixel
      Adoptedapproved by the cat

      1. Install her

        npm install wandering-mochi

      2. Add her to your app

        import { adoptMochi } from "wandering-mochi";

adoptMochi();

        In React, call it inside useEffect and return the result so she moves out cleanly.

      3. Invite her to visit optional

        npx wandering-mochi invite

        Run it, then follow the link it prints. She will visit, review your site and add a stamp to her passport.

      Your signature
      Mochi's paw

      Make her yours

      adoptMochi({ name, owner, photo });

      name renames her, owner is you, and photo is a CSS selector for a picture she will nap under. On this page, that is her passport photo.

      No npm?

      Paste this before </body> instead:

      <script src="mochi.js" defer></script>

      House rules

      Good manners

      She is a cat, not a raccoon.

      How MochiBot works, and how to block her
      • Invitation only. She visits sites that asked for her, and nobody else.
      • Obeys robots.txt. One rule and she stays home.
      • No cookies, no tracking. The cat script remembers how much you love her, on your device, and that's it.
