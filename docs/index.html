    Adopt Mochi

    1. Add one line before </body>:
    2. Add your domain to the guest list with a pull request. She can also find you by herself if a site she visits links to you.
    3. After her first visit, show off your badge: 
      <img src="https://mochi.is-a.bot/badges/your-domain.com.svg" alt="Mochi visited this site" />

    You can personalise her with data-name, data-owner and data-photo, which takes a CSS selector for a picture she will nap under.

    Good manners

    She stays only where she is invited, obeys robots.txt and walks a few steps a day. The cat script uses no cookies and no tracking. Remove the line and she moves out. Read how MochiBot works.
